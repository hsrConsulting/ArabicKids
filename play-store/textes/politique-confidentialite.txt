PRIVACY POLICY — ARABIC KIDS
Last updated: February 18, 2026

---

1. INTRODUCTION

Arabic Kids ("the App") is an educational application designed for children
aged 4-10 to learn the Arabic language. This privacy policy explains how we
handle information when you use our App.

Developer: [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY NAME]
Contact: [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS]


2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Arabic Kids does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

• No account creation on external servers
• No email addresses collected
• No names sent to any server
• No location data collected
• No device identifiers collected or shared
• No analytics or tracking tools

All user data (child name, avatar, progress, scores) is stored LOCALLY
on the device using the browser's localStorage. This data never leaves
the device.


3. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA & GDPR-K COMPLIANCE)

Arabic Kids is designed with children's privacy as a priority:

• We do NOT collect personal information from children
• We do NOT require online registration
• We do NOT have social features, chat, or user-generated content
• We do NOT share any data with third parties
• All learning data stays on the device
• Parents can delete all app data by clearing the app's storage
  or uninstalling the app


4. ADVERTISING

Arabic Kids displays interstitial advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

• Ads are shown periodically between quiz sessions
• We use NON-PERSONALIZED ads only (no ad tracking for children)
• Google AdMob may collect limited device information for ad serving
  as described in Google's privacy policy:
  https://policies.google.com/privacy

We recommend that the ads comply with Google's Families Policy:
https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335


5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The App uses the following third-party service:

• Google AdMob (for advertising)
  - Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  - We request non-personalized ads to protect children's privacy

The App does NOT use:
• Analytics services
• Social media SDKs
• Crash reporting tools
• Any other third-party data collection


6. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

• All user data is stored locally on the device
• No data is transmitted over the internet (except for ad loading)
• The App works fully offline (except for ad display)
• Data can be deleted by clearing app data or uninstalling


7. INTERNET PERMISSIONS

The App requires internet access solely for:
• Loading advertisements (Google AdMob)
• Loading web fonts on first use (Google Fonts)

No user data is ever transmitted.


8. PARENTAL CONTROLS

Parents can:
• Delete their child's profile and progress by clearing app data
• Restrict internet access to disable ads (the app works offline)
• Uninstall the app to remove all local data completely


9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be
reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App
after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.


10. CONTACT US

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at:

Email: [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS]
Developer: [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY NAME]
