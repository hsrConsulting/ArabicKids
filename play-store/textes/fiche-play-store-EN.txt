🌟 Arabic Kids — The fun way for children to learn Arabic!

Want your child to discover the Arabic language? Arabic Kids turns learning into an adventure with interactive lessons, games, and quizzes designed for young learners.

📖 COMPLETE ALPHABET
• All 28 Arabic letters with native pronunciation
• Each letter in 4 forms: isolated, initial, medial, final
• Illustrated example words with emojis for every letter
• Listen and repeat with Arabic text-to-speech

📚 300+ VOCABULARY WORDS
Learn vocabulary across 13 themed categories:
🦁 Animals • 🍎 Fruits • 🥬 Vegetables • 🍕 Food & Drink
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family • 🎨 Colors • 🔢 Numbers • 🧍 Human Body
🏫 School • 🌳 Nature • 🏠 House • 👕 Clothes • 🚗 Transport

Every word comes with an emoji illustration, translation, and audio pronunciation.

🎯 7 QUIZ TYPES
• Letter Quiz: recognize Arabic letters
• Word Quiz: match Arabic words to their translation
• Forms Quiz: identify the different forms of a letter
• Audio Quiz: listen and identify the spoken word
• Categories Quiz: sort words into the right category
• Phrases Quiz: complete simple Arabic sentences
• Match Quiz: connect Arabic words to their translations

🧠 MEMORY GAME
A fun memory game to match Arabic letters with their images!

🏆 PROGRESSION SYSTEM
• Earn points with every lesson and quiz
• Level up: Beginner → Explorer → Champion → Master
• Unlock 15 achievement badges
• Track your alphabet progress (X/28 letters learned)

🌍 MULTILINGUAL
The interface is available in 8 languages:
🇫🇷 French • 🇬🇧 English • 🇪🇸 Spanish • 🇩🇪 German
🇹🇷 Turkish • 🇮🇳 Hindi • 🇮🇩 Indonesian • 🇮🇹 Italian

👶 DESIGNED FOR KIDS
• Colorful, playful interface for ages 4-10
• No online registration required
• Data stored locally on device
• Multiple profiles for families
• Works offline (no internet needed)

📱 ALL SCREEN SIZES
• Phones and tablets
• Portrait and landscape mode
• Notch and gesture navigation support

Download Arabic Kids and give your child the keys to the Arabic language! 🚀
